AI GOT WEIRD - MAKE YOUR PDF SHOW ITS RECEIPTS
Free workflow - checked 16 Oct 2026

1. Open Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM). Add a public PDF or a document you have permission to process.
2. Ask one concrete question instead of requesting a summary of everything.
3. Try this prompt:

Using only this source, answer: [my question]. Give source citations for the important claims. Separate facts from your interpretation. Tell me what the source cannot establish. If the answer is missing, say so.

4. Click each important citation. Read the surrounding passage. Does it support that exact claim, with the same scope and date?
5. Check names, numbers and limitations against the original. A citation is a way to check an answer, not proof it is right.

Real test: public Park et al. Generative Agents paper, arXiv:2304.03442. The party question returned source citations; one was opened and checked. The response also added an unwanted follow-up. This single test is not a reliability benchmark.

Standard access is free with an eligible Google account. Usage limits and region/account requirements apply. No purchase or new plan was required in our test.
